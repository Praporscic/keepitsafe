KeepItSafe Privacy Policy

In short: KeepItSafe keeps your photos, videos, documents, audio and contacts encrypted on your iPhone. We have no servers and no accounts, so we never receive your files, your PIN or pattern, or your encryption keys. The app has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. The only service it talks to is the one that manages your subscription.

What stays on your device

Everything you add to the vault, along with its details such as file names and the app's settings, is stored on your device only. It is encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and the keys are kept in the iOS Keychain. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you.

KeepItSafe has no cloud sync and no backup. If you delete the app or lose your device, the files in your vault are permanently gone.

What we do not collect

  • Your files, photos, videos, documents, recordings or contacts
  • Your PIN, pattern, encryption keys or file names
  • Your name, email address, phone number or location
  • Analytics, usage tracking or crash reports
  • Advertising data. The app shows no ads and does not track you.

Permissions the app may ask for

Each permission is used only on your device, and what you capture goes straight into your encrypted vault.

  • Photos: to import the photos and videos you choose.
  • Camera: to take photos for your vault, and, if you turn on the break-in log, to photograph wrong unlock attempts.
  • Microphone: to record videos with sound and voice recordings. Background recording is only used if you start a recording.
  • Face ID: to unlock the vault. Face ID data is handled by iOS, and the app never receives it.

Subscription

KeepItSafe offers a subscription. Purchases are processed by Apple, and the app uses RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. To do that, RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated for your installation, your purchase and subscription status, and technical information that comes with any internet request, such as your IP address, device type, OS version and app version. No vault content is ever sent. This is the only time the app uses the internet.

RevenueCat handles this data under its own policy: revenuecat.com/privacy. Apple handles your payment under Apple's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Exporting files

If you export a file out of the vault, the exported copy is not encrypted. Any app you save or send it to can keep it, under that app's own privacy practices.

Children's privacy

KeepItSafe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete everything at any time by erasing the vault in the app or by deleting the app. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You can manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings, under your name and then Subscriptions.
