Frequently asked questions

I forgot my PIN or pattern. Can you recover my files?

No. Your PIN or pattern and your encryption keys exist only on your device, so nobody — including us — can unlock or restore your vault. This is what keeps your files private. If you set up Face ID, you can keep using it to unlock.

Where are my files stored? Are they backed up?

Everything is stored encrypted on your device only. KeepItSafe has no account, no servers and no cloud sync or backup. If you delete the app or lose your phone, the files in your vault are gone, so export anything you cannot afford to lose before you do.

How are my files protected?

Files and their details are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM, and the keys are kept in the iOS Keychain. A file is decrypted only while you are viewing it, and the temporary copy is removed when you close the viewer or the vault locks.

Does the app need an internet connection?

The vault itself works fully offline. An internet connection is only needed to confirm your subscription.

The vault erased itself. Why?

Wrong-attempt protection can erase the vault after a set number of wrong PINs or patterns in a row. It is a protection in case your iPhone ends up in the wrong hands. You choose the limit, or can turn it off, in Settings. Erased files cannot be recovered.

What are the decoy vault and the break-in log?

The decoy vault opens with a second PIN or pattern and shows only the files you chose to put in it. The break-in log can photograph wrong unlock attempts so you can see who tried to get in. Both are optional and can be set up in Settings.

Why does the app icon look like a calculator?

You can change the app icon to a calculator, email or printer in Settings, so the vault is less obvious on your home screen. You can switch back to the default icon at any time.

Is exporting a file safe?

An exported copy is not encrypted. Any app you save or send it to can keep it, and the vault can no longer protect it. Export only when you need to.

How do I manage, cancel or get a refund for my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. To manage or cancel, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds directly.
